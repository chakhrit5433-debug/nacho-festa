About Company

Where NachoFesta Came From

Every company has a founding document. Ours is a memory of being hungry — and of somebody deciding that shouldn't continue.

Our Story

Four Moments That Built a Company

Chapter One

A Hungry Childhood

When our founder was a child, he was very poor. There were days when he had nothing to eat at all — days that most people never have to imagine, and that he has never been able to forget.

Chapter Two

A Stranger's Kindness

One day, a kind grandfather made him a warm, crispy, cheesy dish. It was so delicious that he never forgot its taste — even though, back then, he didn't know it was called nachos.

Chapter Three

Chasing the Memory

Years later, after he became successful, he wanted to recreate that special memory — not just the recipe, but the feeling of being handed something warm when you have nothing. That is why he opened Nacho Festa.

Chapter Four

NachoFesta Today

Our mission is to serve not only delicious nachos, but also warmth, happiness and kindness in every bite. Today Nacho Festa is one of the most popular nacho restaurants in Mexico — and every bite reminds our founder of where his journey began.

The Recipe

We Are Still Making That Same Plate

We never tried to reinvent the nacho. We tried to rebuild one specific plate of them — the one handed over a counter to a boy who had not eaten that day.

That is why our portions are large, our cheese is real, and nothing on our menu is designed to be eaten alone. Generosity is not a marketing angle here. It is the entire origin of the business.
